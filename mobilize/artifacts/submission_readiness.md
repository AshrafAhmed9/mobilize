## Closure rules

For every row record the artifact URL/path, product commit, date, reviewer, observed
result, limitations and any remaining action. A completed task or a newly written
paragraph alone does not close an evidence row. Keep Pending, Failed, Passed and
Accepted limitation distinct. Accepted limitation does not mean the capability exists.

Any critical correctness, authorization, data-integrity or submission-access defect
blocks release. Missing user or outcome evidence blocks claims of validated adoption
or real outcome improvement, even if the project can still be submitted honestly.

Before submission the reviewer answers: which objections are now answered by working
evidence, which remain, and whether another implementable change materially improves
competitiveness? Never replace this assessment with invented winning odds.
