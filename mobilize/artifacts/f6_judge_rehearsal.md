A friend of Ashraf's, who had not previously seen or used the project, was

use it without needing help.

**Establishes:** an unguided first-time user, working only from the public
repo, could set up and operate the tool without getting stuck, and formed
a positive impression of the concept and its usefulness.

**Does not establish** (not specifically asked or observed, so not
claimed): whether they understood, at any point, the distinction between
a simulated rehearsal and a real call; whether they encountered a
soft-yes/refused case and understood why it wasn't counted as confirmed;
whether they could locate or reproduce a specific piece of evidence
behind a claim; and no verbatim quote or independent written record exists
from the participant, so their reaction here is Ashraf's paraphrase, not a
direct account.

This is real, honest signal that the setup and core workflow are usable
without hand-holding. It is not the full comprehension check
`validation_protocol.md` Part E describes (the five specific tasks:
launch and explain the mode, run a success scenario and distinguish
